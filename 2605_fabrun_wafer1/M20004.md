# M20004 CHIP COMMENTS

## Layers
- `1/0` Inductors (Al or AlMn)
- `2/0` Capacitors (Nb)
- `3/0` CPW feedline & ground planes (Nb)
- `10/0` Alignment marks (Caltech style: cross in a box)
- `11/0` Dicing crosses
- `20/0` 2" wafer footprint
- `21/0` Square chip footprints
- `22/0` Rectangular chip footprints

## Feedlines
Same design across all chips:
- Central conductor width: 20 $\mu$m
- Gap width: 10.75 $\mu$m
- Ground conductor width: 100 $\mu$m
- Corners use slow-wave design
